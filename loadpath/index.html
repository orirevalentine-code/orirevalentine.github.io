How to play

  1. Drag from a joint (the orange anchors on the cliffs to start) to place a member. Releasing on empty grid creates a new joint. Members can be at most 4 m long.
  2. Road is the deck the truck drives on. It must run continuously from the left cliff to the right one. Steel is cheaper and lighter, so use it for the truss.
  3. Stay under the budget. Right-click or use Delete to remove a member. Undo with Ctrl+Z.
  4. Press Test. The truck is checked at every position across the span. Members show how hard they work: green < 60%, yellow to 90%, orange to 100%, red breaks. All capacities include a safety factor φ = 0.9.
  5. Triangles keep a truss rigid. Each new joint costs money too. Road segments must also carry the truck as a beam between joints, so very long ones bend and snap. From level 3, long members in compression buckle (capacity falls fast with length). From level 5, the deck must not sag more than span/400.
  6. Stars: 3 at or under par (the cheapest design we found), 2 within 25% of par, 1 for any bridge that holds.

Hover or tap any member to see its force and capacity.
